# My 30-day plan

The commitment goes at the top (ch12:168-174):

- **Project:** a one-line name ______
- **Target user:** who specifically needs this ______
- **First deliverable:** the smallest thing you can ship ______
- **Public shipping target:** where it goes live, such as a URL, the App Store, GitHub, or a Reddit post ______
- **Cost budget:** your monthly API and infrastructure ceiling ______

## Week 1: Solidify the fundamentals

My Week 1 project: ______

- Day 1-2: Pick your project. Write the CLAUDE.md. Set up the repo structure.
- Day 3-4: Build the core functionality. Use Claude Code for the heavy lifting. Keep your cost log running.
- Day 5-6: Test, deploy, and polish. Push to production or publish the package.
- Day 7: Write a brief post about what you built and share it somewhere (Discord, Reddit, X). This isn't optional. Sharing is how you get feedback and build reputation.

## Week 2: Go deep on one specialty

My specialty (MCP servers, multi-agent, Micro-SaaS or automation): ______

- Day 8-9: Study the best examples in your chosen specialty. Read source code, not blog posts. If it's MCP, clone three popular servers and read how they handle tool definitions and error cases. If it's multi-agent, map out how existing pipelines coordinate between agents.
- Day 10-12: Build your specialty project. This should be harder than anything in the book. Push yourself into territory where Claude Code can't do everything and you have to think.
- Day 13-14: Document what you learned. Not a polished blog post. Just notes on what worked, what didn't, and what you'd do differently. These notes become your reference material and future content.

## Week 3: Join the community and teach

The communities I join (ch12:164: anthropic.com/discord, r/ClaudeCode): ______

- Day 15-16: Join the Anthropic Discord and r/ClaudeCode. Read the last week of posts. Identify three questions you can answer and answer them.
- Day 17-19: Write and publish one piece of content. A Twitter/X thread walking through a build, a Reddit post sharing your CLAUDE.md config, or a YouTube video of a Claude Code session. Pick the format you're most comfortable with.
- Day 20-21: Help someone in the community with a problem. Not just answering a question, but actually helping them debug or build something. Pair coding via Discord screen share, or a detailed response with code examples. This is how real relationships form in technical communities.

## Week 4: Plan your next 90 days

My 90-day decision (freelance, product, certification or my current job): ______

- Day 22-23: Evaluate your three weeks honestly. What type of work did you enjoy most? What produced the most value? Where do you want to focus?
- Day 24-25: Make your 90-day decision and take the first concrete action. If freelancing: write your profile and pitch three potential clients. If product: set a launch date and tell someone about it. If career: schedule a meeting with your manager or apply to three jobs.
- Day 26-28: Set up your ongoing learning system. A weekly calendar block for Claude Code work. RSS feeds or notification settings for the communities you joined. A project backlog with your next three build ideas. The system matters more than any individual task because it's what keeps you moving after the motivation from this book fades.
